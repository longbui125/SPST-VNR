# Sử Đảng

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build required. Implementation choice delegated by the user's request to design and build the site independently. Local files in E:\VNR; no deployment requested.

## Users
Vietnamese-speaking learners revising the supplied university textbook. Reading, chapter practice, interactive concept maps, and resuming learning without signing in are the core jobs.

## Evidence on Hand
The user's 440-page scan: Bộ Giáo dục và Đào tạo, Giáo trình Lịch sử Đảng Cộng sản Việt Nam (dành cho bậc đại học hệ không chuyên lý luận chính trị), NXB Chính trị quốc gia Sự thật, Hà Nội, 2021, ISBN 9786045765906. Original scanned pages are the visual and factual source. OCR is a search aid, never a replacement for the scan.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Include the introductory chapter, chapters 1–3, conclusion, original review prompts, authored multiple-choice questions with explanations and source pages, interactive mindmaps, chapter progress, anonymous local profiles and progress export/import. Scope of history is the 2021 edition, not events after publication. All historical facts and imagery must be grounded in the source. No invented quotations or generated historical images. Wrong options are authored distractors, not historical assertions.

## Brand Commitments
Vietnamese Party history; archival red and gold, restrained visual hierarchy, authentic printed pages, useful scroll-linked 3D, uncluttered practice and readable maps.

## Product Principles
Source beside the answer. Preserve the complete original. Make learning controls simpler than the presentation. Store progress locally with an explicit limit: browser storage is not cross-device identity. Allow separate profiles on a shared device. Keep sensitive identity out of the product.

## Confirmed redesign

The user rejected the restrained first design. Separate journey, quiz and mindmap routes. Journey is learned directly by scrolling, with strong Three.js/GSAP depth and transitions calibrated to supplied Dribbble references. A more classical archival world is required. Verified external imagery and contemporary decorative graphics are authorized; factual history remains sourced. Existing progress must survive.

## Current user request

Remove the textbook route and OCR/engineering copy from learner-facing UI. Keep concise page citations and image credits. Offer confirmation before clearing only the selected profile’s answers, bookmarks, remembered map nodes and study positions; preserve identity and all other profiles.
